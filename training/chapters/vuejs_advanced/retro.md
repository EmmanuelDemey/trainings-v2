# End-of-day retro

**15 minutes.** One round, everyone speaks.

<div style="display: flex; gap: 2em; font-size: 0.95em;">
<div>

### 1. What I take away

- The one concept that **clicked** today
- Something I will **use on my project** next week

</div>
<div>

### 2. What is still fuzzy

- A notion to **revisit** tomorrow morning
- A workshop step I could not finish

</div>
<div>

### 3. What we adjust

- Pace, depth, live-coding vs. slides
- More practice on a specific topic?

</div>
</div>

<br />

> **ROTI** (Return On Time Invested) — everyone raises 1 to 5 fingers:
> 1 = wasted my day, 5 = far more than expected. Anything below 3 gets a
> "what would have made it a 4?" follow-up.
