# TP 1 — Your digital footprint: a sample

> The expected outcome of workshop 1: a typical footprint, its share of manufacturing, and the two levers compared.

The figures below are **orders of magnitude** for a typical set of devices,
from the ADEME data in the *Impact CO2* tool (check the current values: they are
updated). Yours will differ: use this one to check the **method**.

## The equipment

| Item | Made (kgCO₂e) | Kept | Per year (kgCO₂e) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smartphone | ≈ 85 | 3 years | ≈ 28 |
| Laptop | ≈ 160 | 4 years | ≈ 40 |
| 27" screen | ≈ 300 | 6 years | ≈ 50 |
| TV | ≈ 370 | 8 years | ≈ 46 |
| Internet box | ≈ 40 | 5 years | ≈ 8 |
| **Devices, total** | | | **≈ 172** |

## The uses, over a year

| Use | Per year (kgCO₂e) |
|---|---|
| Video streaming, 1 h a day (France, mostly on a TV or laptop) | ≈ 10 to 25 |
| Video calls, 5 h a week | ≈ 2 to 5 |
| Emails, cloud storage | ≈ 1 to 3 |
| Electricity of the devices (low-carbon French electricity) | ≈ 5 to 10 |
| **Uses, total** | **≈ 20 to 40** |

## The share of manufacturing

About **170 / (170 + 30) ≈ 85 %** of this footprint comes from **making**
the devices. In France, with a low-carbon electricity, the uses weigh little.
In a country whose electricity is mostly coal, the share of use is higher —
but manufacturing remains the largest.

## The two levers

- **Keeping the phone and the laptop two years longer** — the phone goes from
  28 to 17 kgCO₂e a year, the laptop from 40 to 27: **≈ −24 kgCO₂e a year**.
- **Halving the video streaming** — **≈ −5 to −12 kgCO₂e a year**.

Keeping the devices longer wins, by a factor of two or more: it is the first
lever, for a person as for an organisation (the fleet of laptops of a company,
the minimum devices supported by its apps).

## What carbon hides

The ADEME gives a smartphone an **ecological backpack** of **≈ 70 kg** of raw
materials moved, for 150 g in the hand: metals, some of them rare, mined with
water and energy. The depletion of **abiotic resources** is often the first
impact of digital, ahead of carbon.
