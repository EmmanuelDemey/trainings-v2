# Objectives of the training

At the end of the day, you will be able to:

- **Explain** the environmental and social impacts of digital technology, and
  where they come from — mostly **making the devices**
- **Name** the main levers of Green IT, and the ones that matter most
- **Apply** the reference frameworks — **RGESN** and the **INR**'s — to your projects
- **Measure** the footprint of a web page with the right tools
- **Build** an eco-design approach for your organisation: audit, continuous
  monitoring, and the arguments to get it accepted

- **No technical prerequisite**: workshops 01, 02, 03 and 06 need no code
- A **laptop** with a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox
- For workshops 04 and 05, the hands-on ones: **Node.js 22.12+** and **pnpm**,
  and a code editor — the **starter ZIP** of each workshop page if you cannot clone

# A word on figures

The footprint of digital technology is **estimated**, not measured.

- Every study has a **scope**: which devices, which networks, which data
  centres — in the country or abroad?
- Every study has **impact categories**: carbon only, or also metals, water,
  energy?
- Figures **move** as methods improve: the ADEME–Arcep study went from 2.5 %
  to 4.4 % of France's carbon footprint between its 2022 and 2025 editions,
  mostly by counting the data centres abroad that serve French users

<br />

> Quote the **order of magnitude** and the **source**, never a figure alone.
