# What to remember

<div style="display: flex; gap: 2em;">
<div>

### The principles

- Digital is **material**: making the devices weighs the most
- The first lever is the **need**: the greenest feature is the one not built
- Design for **old devices** and **poor networks**
- **Sobriety** before efficiency — beware the rebound effect

</div>
<div>

### In a real project

- Assess with the **RGESN**, act with the **115 practices**, go further with
  the **GR491**
- For AI: the **AFNOR Spec 2314** questions, in order
- **Measure** — EcoIndex, weight, requests — and say what is not measured
- A **budget** in the **CI**, a referent, a ritual

</div>
</div>
