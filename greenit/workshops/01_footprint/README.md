# TP 1 — Your digital footprint

> Estimate the footprint of your own digital equipment and uses, and find out which part weighs the most.

**Duration**: 30 min · **Chapter**: 1 — The environmental impacts of digital

## Goal

Chapter 1 — Most of the footprint of digital technology comes from **making
the devices**, not from using them. This workshop makes you check it on your
own equipment, with public data, before anyone tells you the answer.

There is no code in this workshop, and nothing to run `verify` on.

## Setup

A browser, and the two tools of the ADEME:

- [Impact CO2 — numérique](https://impactco2.fr/outils/numerique): the
  footprint of devices and of uses (video, email, video calls…)
- [Datagir — Que faire de mes objets](https://quefairedemesobjets.ademe.fr/)
  for the end of life, optional

## Steps

1. **List your equipment** — phone, laptop, screens, tablet, TV, box, game
   console, connected objects. For each: its **age**, and how many years you
   expect to keep it.
2. **Its manufacturing footprint** — find each device in Impact CO2, and note
   its kgCO₂e. Divide by the years you keep it: that is its **yearly** share.
3. **Your uses** — estimate a typical week: hours of video streaming, video
   calls, emails sent, cloud storage. Find their footprint in the same tool,
   and bring it to a year.
4. **Compare** — what share of the total is the **manufacturing** of the
   devices? Which device weighs the most per year?
5. **Two levers** — compute the effect of:
   - keeping your phone and your laptop **two years longer**;
   - **halving** your video streaming.

## Deliverable

A small table:

| Item | Footprint (kgCO₂e) | Per year |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop, made — kept 5 years | … | … |
| … | | |
| **Total** | | |

and one sentence: **which lever** would you pull first, and why?

## Checking your work

Nothing automated. You are done when:

- each device has a manufacturing footprint and a yearly share;
- the share of manufacturing in your total is computed;
- the two levers of step 5 are compared, with figures.

Compare with the sample in `solutions/01_footprint/`.

## Going further

- The tool gives carbon only. Look up the **ecological backpack** (in kg of
  materials) of a smartphone in the ADEME data: which impact would carbon alone
  hide?
- Do the same exercise for your **team**: how many devices, of which age?
