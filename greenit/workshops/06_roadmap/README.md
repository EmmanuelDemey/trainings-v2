# TP 6 — Your Green IT roadmap

> Leave the training with a roadmap for your own organisation: a baseline, quick wins, a budget, and the arguments to get it accepted.

**Duration**: 30 min · **Chapter**: 8 — How to convince

## Goal

Chapter 8 — An eco-design approach lasts when it starts small, is measured,
and has an owner. This workshop turns the day into **your** plan, with the
arguments for the people who must agree to it.

There is no code in this workshop, and nothing to run `verify` on.

## Setup

Think of **one** digital service of your organisation — the one you know best.
Keep your notes of workshops 1, 2 and 4 at hand.

## Steps

1. **The service and its baseline** — which service, how many users, and the
   measures you can take this week: EcoIndex and weight of its 3 most visited
   pages.
2. **Three quick wins** — measurable in a month, little effort. For each: the
   measure before, the target after.
3. **One budget** — the limits you would put in the CI (workshop 5).
4. **One product decision** — a feature to remove, a requirement to change
   (supported devices, autoplay, an AI feature…). Who decides?
5. **Your audiences** — for the two people whose agreement you need, the
   argument that speaks to **them** (chapter 8), and the objection you expect.
6. **Make it last** — the referent, the ritual (a review per release?), the
   date of the statement of eco-design.

## Deliverable

One page, the format below, and the **first three actions** read to the room:

```text
Service:            …
Baseline:           EcoIndex …, … kB, … requests (3 pages)
Quick wins:         1. …   2. …   3. …
Budget in the CI:   maxSizeKb …, maxRequests …, maxDom …, minGrade …
Product decision:   …  (decided by …)
Arguments:          <who> — <argument> — <objection and answer>
Referent, ritual:   …
First three actions, with dates:
  1. …
  2. …
  3. …
```

## Checking your work

Nothing automated. You are done when:

- every quick win has a measure before and a target after;
- the product decision names who takes it;
- the first three actions have a date within the next month.

A filled-in example is in `solutions/06_roadmap/`.
